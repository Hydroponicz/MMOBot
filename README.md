# MMOBot: an RPG skilling chatbot for Kick

Viewers type commands in your Kick chat to train skills, collect loot and level up their character. Everything runs on **Kick's official public API** (OAuth 2.1 login, `chat.message.sent` webhooks, and the Chat API for replies) as **one Railway service**. A companion website lets them **log in with Kick** to see their character sheet, inventory, leaderboards and a live activity feed. Progress made in chat syncs automatically, because chat and the website both identify players by their Kick user ID.

## Features

- **16 skills**, each levelling from 1 to 500:
  | Command | Skill | What it does |
  |---|---|---|
  | `!fish` | 🎣 Fishing | Shrimp → Celestial Whale. Every catch gets its own **length and weight** (e.g. `you caught 🐟 Trout (📏 55.3 cm, 1.95 kg)!`, with 🏅 personal bests, 🌟 trophies and 🏆 records flagged), and the reply links to the **Fishing page** (`#/fishing`): the skill's home, where players can also cast at five spots (Harbor Pier, Silverrun River 20, the Open Sea 40, the Abyssal Trench 100, Serpent's Reach 400, each with its own fish) and watch the catch leap out as a procedural 3D model at its real size next to a ruler (and a person for anything over 80 cm). It also has every catch in 3D, a fishdex with personal bests and size ranges, and the records. Sizes: mostly mid-range, about 1 in 40 a trophy up to 25% past the usual max, weight grows with length cubed. Breaking a record with a big fish is announced in chat |
  | `!mine` | ⛏️ Mining | Copper/Tin → Runite ore |
  | `!chop` | 🪓 Woodcutting | Logs → Redwood |
  | `!dig` | 🏺 Digging | Old bones → Dragon relics |
  | `!smelt` | 🔥 Smelting | Turns ores from your backpack into ingots (one ore) and alloys (mixed ores, e.g. copper + tin → bronze) |
  | `!skin` | 🔪 Skinning | Animal hides, from Rabbit to Celestial Fleece. Needs a Skinning Knife in your backpack (shop, 500 pts, or smith it at Smithing 20 from a Sterling Alloy, which is silver + copper ore at Smelting 20) |
  | `!run [course]` | 🏃 Agility | Laps obstacle courses (Village Rooftops at 1 up to Astral Ascent at 500) for XP and action points, no items. Each level makes your stamina refill 0.1% faster: 10% at level 100, 25% at 250, and half the normal time at 500 (twice the actions per hour). Laps sometimes slip (a quarter of the XP), and 5% find a shortcut that costs no stamina. `!agility` works too; `!stamina` shows your refill speed. All three numbers are Economy settings |
  | `!collect` / `!stations` | 🏡 Gathering stations | Farm-style loop for the other gathering skills: 🦀 Crab Pots (Fishing), ⚙️ Ore Drills (Mining), 🌳 Tree Saplings (Woodcutting) and 🏺 Dig Sites (Digging). Everyone starts with one of each; buy more in the shop (750 pts, each one 12% more than the last, up to 100 of each). Every 20 minutes (+5 per 40 levels) each station works one resource you've unlocked, and `!collect` turns everything ready into XP for **one stamina charge**: 20% more XP than gathering the same things by hand, but **no items and no points**, so stations never fill your backpack (Admin → Settings → Economy → Gathering station XP multiplier). `!stations` shows them |
  | `!plant` / `!harvest` | 🌱 Farming | Everyone starts with 1 free plot; buy more (750 pts for the first, each one 12% more than the last, up to 100) and seeds, plant one seed per plot, harvest 1 crop per plot when grown (carrots: 20 min). 16 crops, each feeding another skill (see **Crops** below), with better tiers unlocking up to Farming 456. Planting and harvesting each use a stamina charge per 25 plots (70 plots = 3 charges; with too few charges the rest wait, `plotsPerStamina`). `!farm` shows your plots |
  | `!smith <item>` | ⚒️ Smithing | Turns alloys into weapons and armor, e.g. `!smith bronze sword`. Needs a Smithing Hammer in your backpack |
  | `!lightfire [log]` | 🔥 Firemaking | Burns a log from your backpack (the best one you can, or `!lightfire oak`) for XP and 🌫️ Ashes. Better logs give more XP; each log unlocks at the level it takes to chop it. Needs a Flint and Steel (shop, 50 pts), which lasts 250 fires. Sometimes the fire won't catch: nothing is used up, try again |
  | `!cook [food]` | 🍳 Cooking | Cooks on your fire: `!lightfire` first (it burns 5 minutes, a minute longer per log tier; `!fire` shows what's left). Lighting the fire uses a stamina charge, and cooking on it is free, but each fire cooks at most 10 meals plus 1 per 2 Firemaking levels (`fireMealsBase`, `fireMealsPerLevels`); relight for more. Fish, vegetables, Raw Chicken from fights and the raw meat you get from `!skin` (every skinned animal now gives meat too). Each food unlocks at the level it takes to get it, and better food gives more XP. Food burns sometimes (30% at the food's level, down to 2% 50 levels above it). Cooked food sells for more and `!eat` heals HP |
  | `!craft <item>` | 🧵 Crafting | Leather armor from skinned hides (coif, chaps and body in 10 hide tiers; a bit less defence than metal but adds to your attack with a bow), Large and Huge quivers (1,000 / 2,000 arrows) and Magic Runes (`!craft runes`: 1 Ashes + 1 Tin Ore makes 10) |
  | `!fletch <item>` | 🪶 Fletching | Arrows (`!fletch arrows`: 1 Oak Logs + 1 Feathers + 1 Iron Ingot makes 10; better arrows from better wood and metal), bows (`!fletch oak shortbow`: 2 Oak Logs, 10 bows up to the Elder Bow) and a Quiver (2 Rabbit Hides) |
  | `!saw <item>` | 🪚 Carpentry | Refines materials for Construction. With a 🪚 Saw (500 pts in the shop, or `!smith saw` at Smithing 5 from 2 Bronze Alloy), `!saw planks` turns your best logs into planks (one kind per log, 21 in all, Wooden Planks to Celestial Planks). Nails are made with `!craft nails` (or `!craft iron nails`): a 🔨 Smithing Hammer in your backpack turns a smelted bar into nails (one kind per bar, Bronze to Celestial Nails), also for Carpentry XP. One in, one out, so it never fills the backpack. Shortcuts: `!planks`, `!nails iron`, `!carpentry` |
  | `!build <part>` | 🏗️ Construction | Needs a 🔨 Smithing Hammer. Planks + nails become building parts: `!build frame` (2 planks + 1 nails), `door` (3 + 1), `wall` (Wall Panel, 4 + 2) and `roof` (Roof Truss, 6 + 2) make the best of that part you have materials for, or name one (`!build oak wall panel`). 11 tiers pair a wood with an alloy (Wooden + Bronze at level 1, Oak + Iron 15, Willow + Steel 30 ... Celestial + Celestium 490). Parts are worth more than their inputs and are the materials for player-built structures (houses, shops, towns) to come. Also `!construct` |
  | `!contribute [building] [qty\|all] <part>` | 🏘️ Town | Give building parts to the town (`!contribute 5 walls`, `!contribute forge all frames`). Nine buildings (Sawmill, Great Forge, Harbor, Granary, Tannery, Barracks, Builders' Hall, War Camp for Axes/Spears/Brawling, Shadow Crypt for Daggers/Necromancy) level 1-5 from parts value (3k, 12k, 40k, 120k, 350k pts, admin-scalable); each level gives **everyone** +2% XP in its skills. Unnamed parts go to the lowest building; extra carries over. Titles: the Builder (25k+ given), the Town Founder (top builder). `!town` shows it; the **Town** page (`#/town`) has everything |
  | `!house build` / `!build house` | 🏠 Houses | Build your next house from parts instead of buying it (same character level): Cottage 10 Frames + 8 Wall Panels + 2 Doors + 4 Roof Trusses (Wooden+, Construction 10), House (Oak+, 30), Manor (Willow+, 60), Castle (Yew+, 100), Palace (Ebony+, 150). Cheapest qualifying parts are used first |
  | `!stall build` / `!stall <player>` | 🏪 Player shops | Build a Market Stall (6 Frames + 2 Roof Trusses, Construction 5), then a Shop (Oak+, 40) and an Emporium (Mahogany+, 90): +5/+15/+30 market listings and 1/2/3% off the market fee on your sales, plus a storefront page (`#/town/shop/<name>`) listing your goods |
  | `!brew [potion]` | ⚗️ Alchemy | Brews health and mana potions from farmed crops, from the two herb lines (e.g. 2 Mint → Minor Health Potion, 2 Lavender → Minor Mana Potion), up to the Elixir of Life at 300 (Mandrake Root + Lifebloom + Moonpetal). **Trail Brew** (2 Coffee Beans) restores a stamina charge, once an hour. Also **undead potions** from Ashes plus something dead, which give timed effects when you `!drink` them: Bone Brew (+20% XP, 30 min), Vampire Draught (heal after wins), Wraith Tonic (stamina refills twice as fast, 10 min), Banshee Brew (+25% attack), Grave Luck Tonic (double rare chances) and Lich's Elixir (survive one knockout on 1 HP). `!buffs` shows what's active |
  | `!fight [monster]` | 🗡️ Swords | Fight monsters (Chicken at 1 up to Elder Dragon at 500) for XP and loot. Costs HP. Needs a sword |
  | `!cast [monster]` | 🔮 Magic | Fight the same monsters with a staff (Oak Staff in the shop, or fletch one from logs). Each cast uses a Magic Rune (no backpack slot) and 1 mana. Ten spells unlock with level, from Wind Strike to Starfall, each adding attack. `!heal` also trains Magic |
  | `!cleave [monster]` (`!hack`) | 🪓 Axes | Fight the same monsters with a battleaxe (smithed per metal from 3 bars, or `!buy battleaxe`). Hits harder than a sword and 15% of swings land a second blow, but it's two-handed: your shield doesn't count |
  | `!stab [monster]` (`!backstab`) | 🔪 Daggers | Fight with a dagger (1 bar). Lighter hits, but a quarter are critical hits for double damage, and you sometimes dodge. Every Daggers level adds +0.1% stealth (up to +20%) to `!rob`, `!pickpocket`, `!burgle`, `!poach` and jailbreaks |
  | `!thrust [monster]` (`!lunge`) | 🔱 Spears | Fight with a spear (smithed from 1 bar + 2 planks of the matching Construction tier). Its reach means monsters can't hit you in the first round, and in PvP you always strike first |
  | `!punch [monster]` (`!brawl`) | 👊 Brawling | Fight bare-handed from day one; knuckles (1 bar, or `!buy knuckles`) hit harder. One punch in five stuns: the monster (or player) loses its next swing. `!fight` with no weapon points here |
  | `!raise [monster]` (`!necro`) | 💀 Necromancy | Fight with a scythe (2 bars, two-handed) and the undead you raise: Skeleton at 1 up to the Avatar of Death at 450, each adding attack like a spell. Each fight uses one 🦴 Bone Shard (bagless; `!buy bone shards 50`, or `!craft bone shards` from 1 Raw Chicken + 1 Ashes = 10, Bone Dust 30, Cursed Skull 45, Dragon Bone 150). Your minions take a quarter of every hit |
  | `!shoot [monster]` | 🏹 Archery | Fight the same monsters with a bow. Needs a quiver; each fight uses one arrow, and better arrows add damage. Arrows sit in the quiver (500 max), not the backpack. `!quiver` shows them |
- **Train page** (`#/train`, in the Play menu and on your character page): a button for every one of the 24 skills, for players who'd rather click than type in chat. Each card shows the level, the chat command, and a picker with everything you've unlocked (plus the next unlock, locked); things you can't make yet are marked, every monster is listed for combat, rated for your gear and HP (a fight likely to knock you out asks for a second click, like chat's warning). Each button runs exactly what the chat command runs (`runAction`, `!plant`/`!harvest`, `!cook all`), so stamina, tools, backpack space, HP and loot all work the same. Skills switched off in the admin settings show as off. It also has a **Backpack** for selling without chat (a sidebar that stays in view on wide screens; on phones a bar along the bottom switches between Skills and Backpack, so there's no scrolling past the cards): tap items to pick several (with − / + / All for how many of each) and sell them together, or use **Sell all loot** (= `!sell all`, keeps gear, tools, potions, food and crops), **Sell food** and **Sell crops** buttons (gear, potions and ammo ask before selling). It also covers what keeps you training: when you're hurt or low on mana, a health bar with **✨ Heal** (`!heal`), **🧪 Drink potion** (`!drink`, the potion you need most; the only way up when knocked out) and **🍗 Eat** (`!eat`, the best-fitting food); a **backpack upgrade** button (`!upgrade backpack`, also on the "backpack full" note); and on each gathering card the skill's tool with an **upgrade** button (`!upgrade rod/pickaxe/axe/shovel/furnace`, greyed out until you have the level and points). Your own character page links to it. API: `GET /api/train`, `POST /api/train { skill, target }`, `POST /api/train/sell { item, qty | group | items: [{ item, qty }] }`, `POST /api/train/upgrade { what: 'backpack' | skill }`, `POST /api/train/recover { how: 'heal' | 'drink' | 'eat' }`.
- Target a specific resource you've unlocked: `!mine iron`, `!chop oak`, `!smelt steel`.
- **Bigger hauls**: gathering (fishing, mining, woodcutting, digging, skinning) gives one more item, and its XP, per action every 50 levels: 2 at level 50, 3 at 100, up to 11 at 500, as long as there's backpack room. Rare finds stay single. **Admin → Settings → Economy → Bigger gathering hauls** (0 turns it off).
- **Sell prices follow supply**: every `!sell` adds to channel-wide "sell pressure" for that item, which lowers its shop price (down to 35% of normal at most). The pressure halves every 6 hours, so prices recover on their own. A big sale slides down the curve as it goes, so dumping 5,000 carrots at once pays less than selling them over a day. All crops and the dishes cooked from them share one pool, so switching crops doesn't dodge it. `!price carrot` shows what an item sells for right now, and the **💹 Price checker** on the Market page lists every sellable item with its normal and current price (sort by biggest drops or by what you own). Player market listings aren't affected. Scale, recovery time and floor are in **Admin → Settings → Economy** (scale 0 = fixed prices).
- **XP and levels**: every skill goes from 1 to **500**, with new resources unlocking along the way (e.g. woods: Logs → Birch → Oak → Pine → Willow → Spruce → Maple → … → Celestial Timber at 500). An overall **character level** (up to 120) is based on combined XP, with each skill counting up to its level-120 XP, so no single skill can max it alone.
- **Gear & combat**: smith (or buy) swords and armor in 11 metals, from Bronze (Smithing 1) and Iron (Smithing 15, from Iron Ingots) to Celestial. `!equip` them for attack and defence; weapons need that level in their combat skill to wield (Swords for swords, Axes for battleaxes, and so on), armor needs that Combat level (your best combat skill). There are eight fighting styles, each with its own command and its own trick (see the table above), all fighting the same monsters and balanced to cost about the same HP at par; `!fight` uses your best weapon for the combat skill you're highest in, equipping it if needed. `!equipped` shows your gear, and `!unequip helmet` takes it off. `!sell all` keeps gear and tools.
- **Health and mana**: max HP is 50 + 10 per combat level, max mana 20 + 2 per level. A fight is traded blows: you hit for your level + weapon attack, the monster hits back (less with better armor, much harder the further it outlevels you). Any monster can be fought at any level: one at your level (with matching gear) costs about 10% of your HP, so a stamina bar of fights uses about one meal or one `!heal`; 1.5× your level about 40%; twice your level about 80% and often a knockout. Armor sets and enchants shave that down. At 0 HP you're **knocked out**: no fighting until you're back at full HP (24 hours) or you `!drink` a health potion. You keep all items; you get a little XP for the damage you did. HP and mana refill over time (24h / 1h, set on the admin page). `!hp` shows both, `!drink [potion]` drinks the best potion for the moment, and `!heal` spends 30% of your mana to restore 30% of your HP plus 0.1% per Magic level, up to 60% (it can't revive you); cooked food heals at most 30% of your max HP per meal, however good it is (all three numbers are settings). Every monster is **rated for you** from your level, weapon and armor: ⚪ too easy · 🟢 good match · 🟠 tough · 🔴 hard · ☠️ deadly. `!targets` recommends the best fights for your level, gear and current HP (`!targets bow` for archery), `!monsters` lists them all around your level, `!scout troll` shows how a fight would go (rounds, expected HP loss, XP), and the character page shows the same ratings. A plain `!fight` picks your best safe match; beating something far below you says so and points to `!targets`; and a fight that would likely knock you out (with your current HP) is warned about first. Typing it again within 2 minutes fights anyway. Potions are sold in the shop (150 to 3,500 pts) or brewed with Alchemy, and knock-outs show on the feed and overlay.
- **Crops** (Farming feeds the other skills): seeds are bought in the shop (or `!buy carrot seeds 10`) and don't take backpack space. 15 crop lines with 5 tiers each (tier 2 unlocks 60 Farming levels after tier 1, then +150, +270, +420), plus Coffee. Crops sell for about twice their seed price: the value is in using them, or trading them to players who need them.
  - 🥕 Carrot, 🥔 Potato, 🌾 Wheat (Cooking): roasted, baked and bread dishes, one per tier, that heal more as tiers rise. 🍃 Mint → Lifebloom and 💜 Lavender → Starbloom (Alchemy): health and mana potions.
  - 💠 Flax (Fletching): every bow needs one flax of its tier for the string (two bow tiers per flax tier). ☁️ Cotton (Crafting): cloth robes (hood, skirt, robe) in 5 tiers, light armor that adds to your spell attack when you `!cast`. 🌸 Runebloom (Crafting for Magic): `!craft runes` makes 20 Magic Runes per bloom, +20 per tier, instead of 10 from Ashes + Tin.
  - Boosters, used up automatically when you have one (best tier first): 🌿 Glowmoss bait for `!fish`, 🪨 Stoneroot for `!mine`, 🍄 Truffles for `!dig`, 🟫 Tanbark for `!skin`. Each gives +1 item and +20% rare odds per tier. 🌲 Pine Resin: `!lightfire` burns 2 minutes longer and cooks 5 more meals per tier. 🔥 Emberroot flux: `!smelt` has +15% per tier chance to make two.
  - 🌹 Dye flowers (Rose Madder, Woad, Marigold, Moonlily, Starflower): 3 of a flower unlocks a premium outfit color (Crimson, Indigo, Saffron, Silver, Celestial) on the Customize page for good.
  - ☕ **Coffee** is special: its seeds can't be bought. Gifting subs earns the gifter 1 seed per gifted sub (`coffeeSeedsPerGiftedSub`); each plot grows 2 beans, and 2 beans brew a Trail Brew (+1 stamina charge, once an hour, `trailBrewCooldownMinutes`).
  - The 201 old crops were retired: everything players held (crops, seeds and roasted dishes) was bought back once at its value, and old crops still in plots pay out in points when harvested. Admin settings control a crop growth-time multiplier.
- **Emote shortcuts**: a chat message with the `hydroponiczcobble` emote works like `!mine`, and a target after it works too: the emote then `iron` mines iron (with a space in between). It counts as the Kick emote (sent as `[emote:ID:name]`), typed as `:name:`, or the name as the first word of a message, never the name mid-sentence or in a link. Words after the emote that aren't a target are treated as chat, so it just mines. Other emotes in a message are ignored, so `!mine KEKW` still mines. Add more pairs under **Admin → Settings → General → Emote shortcuts** (`emote=command`, optionally with a fixed target like `cobble=mine iron`), or set the starting list with `EMOTE_COMMANDS`; entries there are cleaned up the same way and bad ones are skipped with a warning in the logs.
- **Shop** (website "Shop" page, or `!shop` / `!buy <item>` in chat): Smithing Hammer 500 points, Bronze Sword 1,000 points, Skinning Knife 500 points, Oak Shortbow 500 points, Quiver 250 points, Flint and Steel 50 points (250 fires), and arrows priced per arrow (Iron 6 up to Rune 110; `!buy arrows 50`, 10 if no amount; they go in your quiver). Prices are editable on the admin page. Logged-in players can also equip, unequip and sell from their character page.
- **Houses** (end game, `!house`, `!house buy`, or the shop's Houses section): homes that add stamina charges, a flat increase to the stamina bar. 🛖 Cottage +1 (150,000 pts, character level 25), 🏡 House +2 (500,000, level 40), 🏘️ Manor +3 (1,500,000, level 60), 🏰 Castle +4 (5,000,000, level 80), 🏯 Palace +5 (15,000,000, level 100). You own one at a time and move up a step at a time, paying the next house's full price. The house shows as a badge on your profile, and moving in is announced in chat. Prices, charges and levels are in **Admin → Settings → Houses** (a price of 0 takes one off sale); **Settings → Events → Houses** switches them off.
- **Backpack**: holds 10 items to start. When it's full, gathering stops until you `!sell`, `!smelt` (always frees space) or `!upgrade backpack`. There are 10 backpack levels, up to 100 slots, and each costs more points than the last.
- **Tools for every skill**: a rod (Fishing), pickaxe (Mining), axe (Woodcutting), shovel (Digging) and furnace (Smelting). Each has 10 tiers. Everyone starts with tier 1, and a new tier unlocks every 50 levels of that skill. `!upgrade rod` / `pickaxe` / `axe` / `shovel` / `furnace` buys the next tier with points (2,000 up to 1,250,000). Better tools fail less often, give bonus XP and improve rare-find odds; better furnaces instead give bonus XP and a chance to smelt two at once. `!gear` shows all your tools; `!rod`, `!axe` and the like show one.
- **Casino** (website "Casino" page, or chat). On the website every game is a **3D scene** on a casino floor (`public/casino3d.js`, three.js): a KICK slot machine whose reels spin and land on the result (lever, chasing lights, coin bursts on wins), a roulette wheel whose ball laps the rim and drops into the winning pocket, a 3D plinko board (pegs, glowing buckets, many balls at once), a blackjack table where cards are dealt from the shoe and the dealer's hole card flips, a rocket flying over a planet for crash (it explodes when it crashes), and a 5×5 mines board of tiles you click to turn over gems or mines. The server still decides every outcome, so chat and the site always agree; browsers without WebGL get the 2D tables. Gamble points on **slots** (Kick-themed reels: 💬 Chat, 💚 Follow, 🎁 Gift Sub, 🎙️ Mic, 🎥 Cam, 🔴 LIVE, 🟩 KICK up to 300x), **roulette** (European wheel: colors, odd/even, halves, dozens or a number), **plinko** (12 rows, low/medium/high risk up to 170x, or **extreme**: 1000x on either edge and nothing in the middle nine; drop up to 1,000 balls at once, the bet is per ball; on the website the Drop button can be spammed, one ball per click with many on the board at once and a running session tally) **blackjack** (3:2 blackjack, dealer stands on 17, double down, and **split** pairs like 8-8 into up to 4 hands), **crash** (a rocket multiplier climbs from 1x until it crashes, up to 1000x; cash out in time to win bet × multiplier) and **mines** (a 5×5 board with 1-24 mines: every gem you find raises the multiplier, cash out any time, hit a mine and lose the bet). In chat: `!slots 500`, `!slots all`, `!roulette red 1k`, `!plinko half high`, `!plinko 100 extreme 50` (50 balls of 100), `!bj 500` then `!hit`/`!stand`/`!double`/`!split`, `!crash 500 2x` (cashes out at 2x), `!mines 500 3` then `!pick 7` and `!cashout`, and `!casino` for help. On the website, crash is played live with a Cash out button (plus an optional auto cash-out); rounds and boards are saved, so a refresh doesn't lose them. Bets accept `500`, `1k`, `half`, `all` or `25%`. Outcomes are decided on the server, and the house edge is similar to a real casino (about 95% return on slots, 97% roulette, 99% plinko, blackjack, crash and mines), so points drain slowly. Big wins (10x or more, or 10,000+ points) show on the feed and OBS overlay. A per-viewer bet cooldown (5s, warns once) stops chat spam; website plinko drops only wait 100ms (Admin → Settings: Plinko website drop cooldown). **Admin → Settings → Casino** opens/closes it and sets the minimum bet, maximum bet and cooldown; `!casino`, `!slots`, `!roulette`, `!plinko` and `!blackjack` can be switched off individually. Points only, no real money.
- **Points**: viewers earn points for chatting (rate-limited), for every action, and by selling loot (`!sell all` or `!sellall`, which keeps gear, tools, seeds, potions, food and crops, so nothing meant for fights or other skills is sold by accident; `!sell all food` and `!sell all crops` sell those groups on purpose). To stop farming, messages under 3 letters, exact repeats and (optionally) brand-new chatters earn no chat points; subscribers (sub badge in chat) earn 2x. All adjustable in **Admin → Settings → General**.
- **Stream events** (**Admin → Events**, timers in **Settings → Events**):
  - **Follows, subs and gifted subs** (Kick webhooks, subscribed automatically): points for the viewer (a follow pays once per viewer), a thank-you in chat (Admin → Settings → Events: **Follow thank-you messages** switches the follow thank-you and its feed/overlay entry off, e.g. during a follow-bot attack; and the **follow flood guard** silences them on its own when more than 5 follows land in a minute, until 10 minutes after the wave), and gifted subs start a channel-wide **double XP** boost (5 minutes per gifted sub, stacking up to an hour). `!boost` shows it. You can start XP or chat-point boosts yourself on the Events page.
  - **💎 KICKs** (Kick's tipping currency, the `kicks.gifted` webhook, subscribed automatically): every KICK pays the sender 2 pts, and bigger gifts set off effects for the whole chat (each tier includes the ones below): **100+** a loot rain (3 pts per KICK shared between up to 10 people who chatted in the last 10 minutes), **500+** double XP for everyone for 10 minutes, **2,500+** wakes the world boss. The bot thanks them in chat (with their gift's name and message), the overlay shows their 3D character cheering, and lifetime totals feed a supporter leaderboard (this week / month / all time) on the Stream page and `!kicks`, plus titles at 100, 1,000 and 10,000 lifetime KICKs (*the Kick Supporter*, *Patron*, *Legend*). Every number, and the thank-you message, is in Settings → Events. KICKs sent before this update aren't counted (the bot only sees new gifts).
  - **Raid bosses**: a giant monster attacks the channel and everyone types `!attack` (with their best weapon, arrows or spells). Its HP scales with how many people are chatting; beat it in time and the reward pool (5,000 pts) is split by damage, everyone gets loot, and the MVP gets rare loot. Start one from the Events page or set it to appear every N minutes. `!raid` shows its HP; the site shows a live health bar (and the OBS overlay with `&raid=1`). Boss HP per chatter is a setting (Settings → Events).
  - **World boss**: a huge boss started from the Events page that stays for days (7 by default) and keeps its HP between streams, with a reward pool 10× a normal raid. Normal raids still run while it's up.
  - **Channel goals**: "chat does 500 mining actions → 2x XP for everyone for 30 minutes". Start one from the Events page, or have one start automatically when the stream goes live (Settings → Events). `!goal` shows progress; the site shows a bar (and the overlay with `&goal=1`).
  - **Random events** while chat is active: a 👺 treasure goblin (first to `!catch` it wins points and a treasure) or a 📦 supply drop (first 3 to `!grab`). Every 15 minutes by default.
  - Raids and random events only run while the stream is live (from Kick's live status), and only while people are chatting. Both can be turned off.
  - **Duels**: `!duel @name [bet]`, then they `!accept` (or `!decline`) within a minute. Fought on copies of both players' HP, so nobody gets hurt for real; the winner takes the bet.

First a **stealth check**: 45%, +0.4% per Agility level you have over the victim, -12% per guard, always 5-90%. Pass it and you take 3% of their points (max 25,000). Fail and you **fight**: both players' combat level, weapon skill and gear count (the PvP combat core below); you fight on your current HP, the victim at full HP plus +15% attack and defence per guard, and they strike first. Win and you take half the haul (your wounds stay); lose and you're left on 1 HP, pay 5% of your own points (100 to 10,000, half to the victim, half removed) and lie low for 30 minutes (their guild scores a war point); a stalemate lets you slip away empty-handed. The fence removes 20% of every haul from the game. A robbed player is safe for an hour, and you can hit the same player once every 6 hours. `!hire 1-3` (or `!guards 1-3`) hires guards for 24 hours; `!hire` alone shows yours and the prices at 0.5% of your points each (min 500), so protecting a big pile costs the most. - **OBS overlay** at `/overlay.html`, kept calm so it doesn't distract viewers: one event at a time (level-ups, rare finds, upgrades, follows, subs, raids, achievements; `&events=all` for every action) fades in on a see-through card, stays a few seconds (`&seconds=6`) and fades out, with the rest waiting in a queue. Boss HP, goals and the rest live on the website; they can be added back with `&raid=1`, `&goal=1`, `&project=1`, `&boost=1` and `&stats=1`, and each event shows the player's own **3D character doing it** (their race, look, gear and cosmetics): swinging a pickaxe at a rock, chopping a tree, casting a line into water, digging, planting, hammering at an anvil, stirring a cauldron or pan over a fire, running, fighting the monster with their weapon (arrows and spells fly to it), cheering on level-ups and achievements, or falling over when knocked out, with bits flying off and the loot popping up. `&scenes=2d` uses the older flat scenes and `&scenes=0` plain text; browsers without WebGL fall back to text. Each 3D view is created when its event fades in and freed when it fades out. The admin page builds the link and has a test button.
- **Busy chat**: when replies queue up, they're sent combined in one message instead of one every 1.2 seconds, so replies don't arrive late.
- **Test mode**: play from the browser (`DEV_MODE=true`) or terminal (`npm run simulate`) without connecting to Kick.

## Tech

Node.js 22.13+ and Express, with SQLite built into Node (a single file, no database server to run). The frontend is plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step. The only npm dependency is `express`.

```
src/
  server.js        app wiring
  config.js        all settings (from .env)
  db.js            SQLite schema + queries
  game/skills.js   ← all game content: skills, items, XP, unlock levels, prices
  game/xp.js       level curve
  game/engine.js   chat handling, player profiles and the guide
  game/features/   game rules by area: skilling, combat, vitals (HP/mana/potions), shop,
                   farming, info, casinoGames, events (raids, duels, follows/subs),
                   museum, progression (dailies, achievements, seasons, trading)
  bot/             Kick API client, webhook receiver, reply queue
  web/             Kick login (OAuth), sessions, JSON API
public/            website + OBS overlay
railway.json       Railway build/deploy settings
```

## Quick start (local, no Kick needed)

```bash
npm install
cp .env.example .env        # then set DEV_MODE=true in .env
npm start
```

Open http://localhost:3000 and use **Test chat** to try `!fish`, `!mine copper`, `!mine tin`, `!smelt`, `!stats`, `!sell all`. Run `npm test` to run the test suite.

### Checking the game

- `npm test` includes a **fuzz test**: players typing random commands (and using the market, guilds and bounties) over weeks of game time, checking that points and items always stay valid. `FUZZ_SEEDS=40 FUZZ_STEPS=6000 node --test test/fuzz.test.js` runs a much longer hunt.
- `npm run balance -- --players 40 --days 28 --streams 4 --hours 3` is a **balance simulator**: simulated viewers with different play styles (gatherers, crafters, fighters, farmers, gamblers; casual to grinder) play weeks of streams with the real game rules and your current settings (set env variables like `STAMINA_MAX=5` to try changes first). It reports levels and points per week and style, rare finds, pets, and where points come from and go. `--out report.md` saves it.
- `npm run loadtest -- --chatters 200 --seconds 60 --rate 6 --viewers 30` is a **load test**: hundreds of chatters sending commands while overlay and site viewers stay connected and browse. It reports time per message, website response times, event-loop delay and memory, using a throwaway database.

## Deploying on Railway (one service)

The website, API, webhook receiver and bot all run in a single Node process, and the database is a SQLite file on a Railway volume. You don't need a separate database service. `railway.json` sets the start command, the health check (`/healthz`) and the restart policy.

### 1. Create the service
1. Push this repo to GitHub, then in Railway choose **New Project → Deploy from GitHub repo** and pick it.
2. Open the service → **Settings → Networking → Generate Domain**. You'll get something like `mmobot-production.up.railway.app`. The app picks the domain up automatically from `RAILWAY_PUBLIC_DOMAIN`.
3. **Attach a volume**: right-click the service (or use the command palette) → **Attach volume**. Any mount path works, e.g. `/data`. The database is stored there automatically via `RAILWAY_VOLUME_MOUNT_PATH`. Without a volume, all player progress is lost on every redeploy, and the logs and admin page warn about it.

Keep the service at **1 replica**. SQLite lives on the volume, and Railway volumes attach to a single instance.

### 2. Create a Kick app
On **kick.com → Settings → Developer**, create an app:
- **Redirect URL**: `https://<your-domain>/auth/callback`
- **Enable webhooks**: on, with **Webhook URL** `https://<your-domain>/webhooks/kick`
- **Scopes**: `user:read`, `chat:write`, `events:subscribe`

Copy the **Client ID** and **Client Secret**.

### 3. Set the variables
In the service's **Variables** tab:

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| `KICK_CLIENT_ID` | from your Kick app |
| `KICK_CLIENT_SECRET` | from your Kick app |
| `KICK_CHANNEL` | your channel name (the part after `kick.com/`) |
| `PUBLIC_URL` | *(optional)* your own domain, e.g. `https://mmo.yourchannel.com` (a bare `mmo.yourchannel.com` works too). Without it, Railway's generated domain is used |

Everything else is optional (see `.env.example`). `PUBLIC_URL`, `DB_PATH` and `SESSION_SECRET` are worked out automatically. Railway redeploys when you save.

### 4. Go live
1. On startup, the app uses an **app access token** to look up your channel and **subscribe to its chat** (`chat.message.sent`). It re-checks every 30 minutes, because Kick drops subscriptions whose webhook fails for over a day. Chat commands start working straight away and progress is tracked.
2. Log into your site with **Log in with Kick** as your streamer account. The `KICK_CHANNEL` account is admin automatically.
3. **Connect the bot account** that replies come from, e.g. a Kick account called `mmobot`. Kick's login page always uses whichever account is logged into kick.com in that browser, so:
   1. On **Admin → Bot account**, click **Get bot login link** and copy it. The link works once and expires after 30 minutes.
   2. Open a **private/incognito window**, paste the link, log into Kick as `mmobot` and approve.
   3. In your chat, type `/mod mmobot` so slow mode and follower-only mode don't block its replies.

   The site refuses your channel account as the bot account. Without a bot account, replies go out through **Admin → Channel connection** as your Kick app's bot (the API's `type: "bot"`).
4. Type `!fish` in your chat. 🎣

The admin page shows the chat subscription, message and reply counts, storage status and the last error.

Webhook requests are verified against Kick's signature (RSA-SHA256 over `message-id.timestamp.body`). The public key is fetched from the API, with the published key built in as a fallback. Duplicate deliveries are ignored.

### Using your own domain
1. In Railway, open the service → **Settings → Networking → Custom Domain**, add your domain (e.g. `mmo.yourchannel.com`) and create the DNS record it shows you at your domain provider.
2. Set `PUBLIC_URL` to that domain in the service's **Variables** tab. The Kick login redirect, the webhook URL, the links the bot posts in chat and the overlay link all use it. **Admin → Settings** shows the site URL in use and where it came from.
3. On kick.com → Settings → Developer, change your app's **Redirect URL** to `https://<your-domain>/auth/callback` and **Webhook URL** to `https://<your-domain>/webhooks/kick`. Kick only accepts the URLs registered there, so login and chat stop working until they match.

The older `BASE_URL` variable still works; `PUBLIC_URL` wins if both are set.

### 5. Add the overlay (optional)
In OBS, go to **Sources → + → Browser** and paste `https://<your-domain>/overlay.html`, with width 400 and height 600. It shows "MMOBot overlay connected" for a few seconds when it loads, then every action as it happens, with level-ups, rare finds and upgrades highlighted. **Admin → Overview → OBS overlay** builds the link for you: every action or only the big moments (`?events=big`), how long each stays on screen and how many show at once. Its **Send test event** button pops a sample message onto every open overlay, so you can check your OBS setup without anyone playing.

### Testing with real Kick chat on your computer
Kick can only deliver webhooks to a public URL. Run a tunnel (e.g. `cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:3000`), set `PUBLIC_URL` to the tunnel URL in `.env`, and point a second Kick app's redirect and webhook URLs at it. Or just deploy to Railway and test there.

## Customizing

- **Game content**: edit `src/game/skills.js` to change items, XP values, unlock levels, sell prices, rare-drop odds and fail messages. The website's guide page updates automatically.
- **Pacing and prices**: change them on **Admin → Settings**. They take effect immediately and are stored in the database. The `STAMINA_MAX`, `STAMINA_MINUTES`, `CHAT_POINTS` and similar variables only set the starting defaults. Change the level curve in `src/game/xp.js`.
- **Tools**: prices and stats are editable live on **Admin → Settings**. Tool names and icons are in each skill's `tool` block in `skills.js`, and the shared price/stat ladder is `TOOL_LADDER`.
- **Level caps**: set `maxLevel` on a skill (all current skills use 500; the default is 99).
- **New skill**: add an entry to `SKILLS` in `skills.js` (with `command`, `resources` and `rares`), add its items to `ITEMS`, and add a color variable `--<skillid>` in `public/styles.css`. Existing players get the new skill automatically.
- **Quiet mode**: `REPLY_IN_CHAT=false` stops the bot from replying in chat. Progress still tracks and shows on the site and overlay.
